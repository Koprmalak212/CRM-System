1. BASIC IDEAS
===============================================================================

Two kinds of people exist in the system. Keep them apart:

  STAFF (also called "team members" or "admins")
    The people who sign in and work in the system. Each has an account with a
    password, a role (admin or user) and an integrations permission.

  USERS (customer users)
    The people who work at your customer companies. They are records in the
    database: a name, an email address, a role (admin, editor, support or
    viewer, which describes their role at the customer), a country, a status
    and a company. They do NOT sign in and cannot see the system.

How the data connects:

  Company --< Users                 a company has many people
  Company --< Invoices              invoices belong to a company (and name a
                                    customer contact)
  Company --< Appointments          the calendar items
  Company or User --< Notes         notes can be about either
  Company or User --< Tickets       a ticket is about a person and/or a company
  Ticket --< Comments               the discussion on one ticket
  Invoice --< Lines and Payments

Everything that is created, changed or deleted is also written to the
activity log, and shown in the timeline of the person or company it concerns.

2. SIGNING IN, SIGNING OUT, FIRST STEPS
===============================================================================

Sign in (auth/login.php)
  Enter username (or email) and password. After 5 wrong tries for one name,
  or 15 from one address, sign-in is paused for 15 minutes.
  If you have two-step sign-in switched on you then enter a 6-digit code from
  your authenticator app, or one of your recovery codes.
  You are signed out automatically after 30 minutes of inactivity, or after
  8 hours in total. A message tells you why.

Forgot your password (auth/forgot.php)
  Enter your email address. If an account exists you receive a link that works
  for one hour and one time. The page gives the same answer whether or not the
  address exists. Choosing a new password signs you out everywhere else.
  Until an email server is set up (Settings > Email), the message is not
  delivered but stored in the outbox on that tab, where an admin can open it.

First sign-in with a temporary password
  When an admin creates your account with a temporary password you must choose
  your own password straight away (auth/change_password.php).

Invitation (auth/invite.php)
  An admin can invite you by email instead. You open the link, choose a
  password and your account is ready.

Sign out
  Use the sign-out button; the session ends at once.

Passwords must have 12 to 256 characters and may not contain your username or
email. A long sentence is better than a short complicated word.

3. THE LAYOUT
===============================================================================

Sidebar
  Overview, Users, Companies, Calendar, Tickets, Connections, Uptime,
  Invoices, Reports, Backups and Activity logs, with Settings reached from
  its own link. It pops out when you
  hover, and can be pinned open with the pin button; the choice is remembered
  in your browser. On a phone it becomes a menu.

Top bar
  - The search box (see 11).
  - The notification bell with the unread count (see 13).
  - The language selector (see below).
  - The light / dark switch. By default it follows your computer's setting.
  - The sign-out button.

Language
  The language selector uses Google Translate. The first time you pick a
  language you are asked for permission, because the page text is sent to
  Google. Nothing is sent before you agree. You can withdraw the permission in
  the footer.

Phones and small screens
  Below 640 px wide, tables become stacked cards (one card per row, each value
  with its label) so you never scroll sideways. The calendar month grid is
  hidden on phones and the agenda list is shown instead.

Tables everywhere
  Most lists have search, filters (usually as pills above the table), sortable
  column headers (click once to sort, again to reverse) and pages.

Status badges
  Colours always come with words (for example "Overdue", "Done"), so meaning
  never depends on colour alone.

4. OVERVIEW (index.php)

The first page after sign-in.

  - Health: whether the main parts (database, monitoring, backups) are fine.

5. USERS (users/users.php, users/user.php, users/user_new.php)
===============================================================================

Users list
  Shows name, company, role, status, last seen and number of logins.
  - Search by name or email.
  - Filter by status (active, pending, suspended), role and company.
  - Sort by name, company, role, status, last seen or logins.
  - "Clear filters" resets everything.
  - Add user opens the new-user form.

New user (users/user_new.php)
  Name, email (must be unique), role, country, company (picked from your
  companies, or left empty) and status.

User page (users/user.php)
  Everything about one person in one place:
  - Details with edit, and suspend / activate.
  - Their company (link to it).
  - Notes and the timeline (see 7).
  - Their tickets with a button to open a new one for them.
  - "Appointments of <company>": the upcoming appointments of their company,
    with the ones where they are the contact marked "You are the contact".
  - Invoices where they are the contact.
  - Their connections (sessions).

Suspending
  A suspended user stays in the system but is marked suspended everywhere. Use
  it instead of deleting when a person leaves a customer but history matters.

Deleting
  Possible after a confirmation. Tickets of that person remain; appointments
  remain and only lose their contact.

6. COMPANIES (companies/companies.php, companies/company.php, companies/company_edit.php)
===============================================================================

Companies list
  Name, location, KvK number, number of users, open invoices and open tickets.
  Search, sort and pages as usual.

Company form
  Name, address, postcode, city, country, KvK number, VAT (BTW) number, email,
  phone, website and notes. Fields are cleaned (spaces, length) when saved.

Company page
  - Details and the address as it appears on invoices.
  - The people who work there, with a link to add one.
  - Invoices of that company, with a button to create one with the company

  - Open tickets of the company.
  - Notes and timeline.
  - Upcoming appointments, with a button to add one and a link to download

Deleting a company
  Needs a confirmation. It is refused while real invoices are linked to it,
  because invoices must be kept. People of the company stay but lose the
  company link; appointments of the company are deleted with it.

7. NOTES AND THE ACTIVITY TIMELINE
===============================================================================

Where
  On every user page and every company page.

Notes
  Pick a kind (Note, Call, Email or Meeting), type up to 2000 characters and
  save. Each note shows who wrote it and when. You can edit it (it then shows
  "edited") or delete it after a confirmation.

Timeline
  Notes and system events (ticket opened, invoice created, appointment
  added, and so on) in one list, newest first, so you can see the whole story
  of a customer in one glance.

Tip
  Write facts and agreements, not opinions. Do not put sensitive personal
  details (health, religion, ID numbers) in notes.

8. TICKETS (tickets/tickets.php, tickets/ticket.php, tickets/ticket_new.php)
===============================================================================

A ticket is one support case.

Fields
  Title, description, the contact (a user), the company (filled in from the
  contact), priority (Low, Normal, High, Urgent), status (Open, In progress,
  Waiting, Resolved, Closed) and an assignee (the staff member responsible).

Tickets list
  - Filter by status, priority, assignee and company, and search by text.
  - Sort by newest, priority, status or last update.
  - Badges show priority and status.

Ticket page
  - Details you can edit (status, priority, assignee).
  - The comment thread: add comments; each shows author and time.
  - Links to the contact person and the company.
  - Closing or resolving records the time, which feeds the "time to resolve"
    chart on the Overview.

Urgent tickets
  A ticket with priority Urgent creates a notification (the bell) and an
  email once.

New ticket
  From the Tickets page, or from a user or company page with the person or
  company already filled in.

SLA (service level)
  Every new ticket gets two moments: when the first response is due and when
  the solution is due. They follow from the priority (Settings > Tickets;
  out of the box Urgent 30 minutes and 4 hours, High 2 and 8 hours, Normal
  8 and 24 hours, Low 1 and 3 days). The clock runs 24 hours a day.
  - First response: the first comment a staff member writes, or a staff
    member changing the status. System notes and customer emails do not count.
  - Waiting: while the status is Waiting (for the customer) the clock stands
    still. That time is added to the resolve target when the ticket moves on.
  - A contract can give one company shorter targets (see 10B).
  - The list has an SLA column with a countdown, and a filter for tickets
    past a target, about to breach, or escalated. The ticket page has an SLA
    box with both targets.
  - About to breach: with less than a quarter of the time left a
    notification is raised once. Breached: another one when the moment
    passes. A ticket that misses its SOLUTION target is escalated: it is
    marked, and a note is added to its thread.
  - The Overview shows "SLA met, 30 days": of the tickets resolved in the
    last 30 days, the share that was resolved in time.
  - Tickets from before this feature have no targets ("No target") and are
    left out of the numbers.

Email to ticket
  Mail sent to your support address becomes a ticket (Settings > Tickets,
  and the job bin/poll_inbox.php, see 23).
  - A new message opens a ticket. A sender who is a known user is linked to
    that person and their company; the general address of a company links
    the company. The sender gets one confirmation with the reference.
  - A reply with the reference in its subject, for example [TCK-0042], is
    added to that ticket as a comment marked "by email", without the quoted
    earlier mail. It reopens a resolved ticket and ends "Waiting".
  - Only the requester, the contact person or a colleague of the same
    company can add to a ticket. Anyone else gets a new ticket.
  - On the ticket page, tick "Also email this reply to ..." to send your
    comment to the customer, signed with your name and the company name.
  - Automatic mail (out-of-office, bounces, newsletters) is ignored.
    Attachments are not stored.

9. CALENDAR AND APPOINTMENTS
===============================================================================

Calendar page (calendar/calendar.php)
  - A month grid, Monday first. Appointments appear on the day they happen;
    one that lasts several days appears on every day. A busy day shows three
    and "+N more".
  - Previous, next and "today" buttons; invalid months fall back to this
    month.
  - A company filter to see one customer's appointments only.
  - Clicking a day starts a new appointment on that date.
  - Below the grid, the agenda lists every appointment of the month with time,
    company, contact, place and status. On a phone only the agenda is shown.
  - Download as .ics for any calendar app.

Appointment form (calendar/appointment_edit.php)
  Company (required), contact person (optional, must work at that company),
  title, location, notes, start and end (an appointment may last up to 14
  days) and status (Scheduled, Done, Cancelled).
  Impossible dates are refused.

Edit and delete
  Open an appointment to edit it. Cancelling keeps it in the calendar; deleting
  asks for confirmation and removes it.

Who sees what
  The calendar and the appointment forms are for staff. Customer users do not
  sign in. What a "user" sees is a panel on their own page (and the company
  page) in the dashboard, so staff can answer "what is planned with this
  customer?" quickly:
  - Upcoming appointments of the person's company, not cancelled ones.
  - The ones where they are the contact are marked "You are the contact".
  - A colleague at the same company sees the appointment without the mark.
  - A person at another company, or without a company, sees none of them.

.ics export (calendar/calendar_ics.php)
  One appointment, or all of a company's appointments from 30 days ago on, as
  a file that opens in Outlook, Google Calendar, Apple Calendar and others.
  Times are local times; special characters are safe.

10. INVOICES, PAYMENTS, PDF AND RECURRING INVOICES
===============================================================================

Invoices list (invoices/invoices.php)
  - Summary tiles: open amount, overdue amount, collected in the last 30 days.
  - Filters: by state (open, due soon, overdue, paid) and by date range.
  - Search by number, customer or email; sort by the column headers.

New invoice (invoices/invoice_new.php)
  - Pick a company (fills in name and address) or type a customer.
  - Send-to email, customer address, issue date, an optional supply date,
    payment terms (which set the due date), VAT rate and notes.
  - Lines: description, quantity, unit price. Totals are calculated by the
    server in whole cents: subtotal, VAT and total.
  - The invoice number is created automatically and is unique.

Invoice page (invoices/invoice.php)
  - All details and lines.
  - Record a payment: amount, method (iDEAL, Bank transfer, Credit card,
    Direct debit), date, reference. When payments cover the total, the invoice
    shows as paid; partial payments leave it open.
  - Download PDF.

Statuses
  Open (not yet due), Due soon (due within 7 days), Overdue (past the due date and
  not paid), Paid.

PDF (invoices/invoice_pdf.php)
  A professional layout made inside the system (no outside service). It
  shows your supplier details, the customer, lines, VAT and total, the due
  date, and payment details. Your BTW number and IBAN come from
  Settings > Company details; if they are empty they are simply left off.

Recurring invoices (invoices/recurring.php)
  For fixed monthly, quarterly or yearly work.
  - Create a schedule: company, description, amount, VAT, frequency (Month,
    Quarter, Year), first date, payment term.
  - The list shows the next date and the two after it, so you can check the
    pattern.
  - Pause or resume. Periods that passed while a schedule was paused are
    skipped, not billed afterwards.
  - Invoices are created by the daily job (bin/run_recurring.php, see 23). Never
    twice for the same period, even if the job runs twice.
  - A generated invoice is an ordinary invoice that you can open, send and
    record payments on.
  - Services that belong to a contract do not need a schedule: see 10B.

10A. QUOTES (quotes/quotes.php)   [module: Quotes]
===============================================================================

A quote (offerte) is an offer to a customer. It has the same lines as an
invoice, a date until which it is valid, and an outcome.

  - New quote: choose a company (fills in name, email and address) or type
    the customer by hand, add the lines, pick the BTW rate. It is saved as
    a draft with a number such as OFF-2026-1001.
  - Download PDF gives the quote in the same layout as an invoice, without
    payment lines.
  - Outcome: Mark as sent, Accepted by the customer, or Declined (with an
    optional reason). An accepted or declined quote can be reopened as a
    draft, unless it already became an invoice or a contract.
  - Expired: a draft or sent quote whose "valid until" date has passed. It
    counts as lost. Edit it and set a new date to offer it again.
  - Turn it into an invoice: one click copies the customer and the lines
    into a new invoice dated today, payable in 14 days. Only once.
  - Turn it into a contract: one click makes a DRAFT contract whose monthly
    services are the lines of the quote. Check the dates and the invoicing,
    then make it active. Only once.
  - The four numbers on top: what is open, what was won, what was lost, and
    the win rate (accepted quotes out of all quotes with an outcome).

  Example: you quote 10 seats of 3CX. The customer says yes: click
  "Accepted", then "A contract". Set the start date, make it active, and the
  first invoice follows on the date you chose.


===============================================================================
10B. CONTRACTS AND SUBSCRIPTIONS (contracts/contracts.php)   [module: Contracts]
===============================================================================

A contract says which services one company has and what they cost a month.

  - Lines: one per service. Choose the kind (Fiber / internet, 3CX seats,
    Yeastar seats, Security, Phone numbers, Other), a description, the
    quantity (for seats: the number of seats) and the price per unit per
    month, excluding BTW.
  - Term: a start date and an end date. Leave the end date empty for a
    contract that runs until you end it.
  - Renews automatically: on the end date the contract goes on for the
    renewal period (12 months unless you change it). Without the tick it
    ends that day.
  - Renewal alert: so many days before the end date (60 unless you change
    it) the contract is marked "Renewal due", a notification is raised once,
    and the Overview lists it under Needs attention.
  - Invoicing: every month, quarter or year, or not from the contract. The
    contract then creates its own invoices on the scheduled day, with one
    line per service. A quarterly invoice is three months. A period is never
    invoiced twice. With the Invoices module off nothing is invoiced.
  - Status: Draft (no invoices, no alerts), Active, Ended. End contract
    stops it today; the invoices it made are kept.
  - SLA of the contract (optional): a response and a resolve target in
    hours. They apply to the tickets of that company whenever they are
    shorter than the standard targets.
  - The four numbers on top: revenue per month and per year of all active
    contracts, the number of active contracts, and what is inside its
    notice period.
  - The company page shows the running contracts and what the company pays
    a month.
  - The daily job is bin/run_contracts.php (see 23). The same work is also
    done when the site is opened.

11. GLOBAL SEARCH (search/search.php)
===============================================================================

Type in the search box on any page and press Enter. Results are grouped:
Users, Companies, Invoices, Tickets, Quotes, Contracts, each with the matching
text and a link.
Characters like % and _ are searched literally, so searching "50%" finds
"50%". Searches that are too frequent are slowed down.

(There are two hidden surprises in the search box. They are left for you to
find.)

12. CSV EXPORT AND IMPORT (admin only)
===============================================================================

Export (system/export.php)
  Download Users, Companies, Invoices, Tickets or Activity logs as CSV
  (UTF-8, opens correctly in Excel). Available from the list pages and from
  the logs page. Every export is logged.

Import (system/import.php)
  Upload a CSV of Users or Companies (up to 200 KB and 1000 rows).
  - Rows that already exist (same email or company name) are skipped.
  - Rows with problems are not imported; a list shows the row and the reason.
  - A summary tells you how many were added, skipped and rejected.
  Tip: export first to get the right column layout, and test with a few rows.

Safety
  Cells beginning with = + - @ are made harmless so a spreadsheet cannot run
  them as formulas.

13. NOTIFICATIONS AND EMAIL
===============================================================================

The bell
  Shows the number of unread notifications. The system checks for new events
  about every three minutes while you work. Events:
  - An invoice became overdue.
  - A backup failed.
  - A monitored service went down.
  - A ticket was set to Urgent.
  Each event appears once. Clicking a notification opens the related page and
  marks it read. The notifications page lists them all and can mark all read.
  Notifications older than 90 days disappear.

Notification emails
  The same events are emailed once, to:
  - the notification address (Settings > Email, EMAIL_TARGET), and
  - each staff member who ticked "email me notifications" in Settings >
    Account.

Email settings (Settings > Email) - fill these in yourself
  SMTP_SERVER    the address of your mail server
  SMTP_PORT      usually 587 (STARTTLS) or 465 (SSL)
  SMTP_USERNAME  the mail account
  SMTP_PASSWORD  its password (stored encrypted)
  EMAIL_FROM     the sender address shown to receivers
  EMAIL_TARGET   where notification emails go
  Also: the website address used in links inside emails. The same names can be
  set as environment variables. What you save on the page wins.
  The button "Send a test email" sends a message to your own address to check
  the settings.

Outbox
  Every email is recorded first (recipient, subject, text, status Recorded,
  Sent or Failed) and kept 30 days. Without an email server nothing is sent
  and the outbox is where you read reset and invitation links while testing.

14. CONNECTIONS (monitoring/connections.php)
===============================================================================

A list of sessions and devices: user, IP address, device, location, protocol

Use it to spot odd access, such as a flagged connection from an unexpected

===============================================================================
15. UPTIME AND ENDPOINT CHECKS
===============================================================================

Uptime (monitoring/uptime.php)
  A status page in the style of Uptime Kuma: each service with its current
  state (up, slow, down), recent history bars and response time, and an
  overall banner.
  The data comes from your Uptime Kuma server. Connect it in Settings > Kuma
  API Key (address and key). A service that goes down triggers a
  notification. Heartbeat history is kept 30 days.

Endpoint checks (monitoring/monitoring_uptime.php, bin/check_endpoints.php)
  Your own checks of web addresses: add an address, the method (GET or HEAD)
  and the expected answer. The system calls it on a schedule and records up,
  slow or down with the time taken. Addresses that point to internal or
  private networks are refused on purpose.


===============================================================================
15A. CALLS, MESSAGES AND SHORT LINKS (CONNECTORS)
===============================================================================

Connect your phone system and other services under Settings > Connectors
(admins only): 3CX and Yeastar (call history), Twilio (calls and text
messages) and Short.io (short links). Each service has its own box: the
credentials (secrets are stored encrypted and never shown again), a "Test
connection" button, "Save", "Sync now" and "Remove credentials". The data is
fetched every few minutes by a scheduled job, or at once with Sync now.

Calls page (calls/calls.php)
  Every call and message, newest first, with the number of calls and missed
  incoming calls in the last 30 days. Filters: type (calls or messages),
  service (3CX, Yeastar, Twilio), company, person, direction, status, a date
  range, and a search on the number or the message text. Sort by when, length,
  status or service. A number that belongs to nobody on file shows as
  "Unmatched"; the company filter has an "Unmatched numbers" choice.

How a number finds its person
  The last nine digits are compared with the phone numbers of people and
  companies, so +31 6 1234 5678, 0031 6 1234 5678 and 06 1234 5678 are one
  number. One person gives that person and their company; colleagues sharing a
  number give the company; a company's own number gives the company.

Short links page (links/links.php)
  Links made with Short.io, filterable by company, date and text. Anyone who
  is signed in can make a short link (address, optional title, optional
  company); it is created at Short.io and stored here. Admins can sync.

On the company and person pages
  A box "Calls and messages" (the latest five, with a link to all of them,
  already filtered) and, on a company, a box "Short links".

Privacy
  Message text is only stored when "Keep the text of messages" is ticked for
  Twilio. Calls and messages are deleted after 12 months, are included in the
  privacy export, and the privacy erasure unlinks the person and blanks their
  number, matching key and message text.

18. ACTIVITY LOGS (system/logs.php)

The audit trail: time, level (info, warning, error, critical), who, what,

  - Search and filter by level.
  - Export as CSV (admin).
  - Clear entries older than a chosen number of months (admin); this too is

19. SETTINGS (settings/settings.php)
===============================================================================

Account (everyone)
  - Change your password.
  - Two-step sign-in: scan the QR code or enter the key in an authenticator
    app, confirm with a code, and save your 10 recovery codes. Turn it off

Integrations (permission needed)

Team & access (admins) - see 20.

Company details (admins)
  Your own company name, address, KvK, BTW number and IBAN for PDF invoices.
  The IBAN is checked for correctness before saving.

Email (admins)
  The SMTP settings, notification address and the outbox (see 13).

Kuma API Key (admins)
  Server address and key of Uptime Kuma, with a "Test connection" button.

Tests
  The documented list of every test and fix, with result (pass, fixed, open,
  info), what was checked and notes. Useful as proof of quality and for
  finding open points. New entries can be added by hand.


===============================================================================
19A. MODULES (Settings > Modules, admins)
===============================================================================

Eleven parts can be turned on or off for each install: Calendar, Tickets,
Invoices, Calls & messages, Short links, Connections, Uptime, Health checks &
API, Reports, Backups and Activity logs. Tick the ones this install uses and
press Save. Users, companies, notes, search, notifications and the settings
are always on.

What happens to a module that is off
  - Its menu item and its pages are closed: a page says "This part is turned
    off" and an API address answers with a short error.
  - Its search results and notifications disappear, and so do its background
    jobs (the scripts print one line and exit).
  - Its settings tab disappears too: Uptime Kuma goes with Uptime, Connectors
    with Calls or Short links, and Integrations with Health checks & API.
  - Nothing is deleted. Turn the module on again and everything is back.

Modules do not depend on each other, so any combination works. Each change is
written to the activity log as "Modules changed".

20. TEAM MANAGEMENT (Settings > Team, admins)
===============================================================================

  - Add a team member: name, username, email, role, integrations permission.
    Either set a temporary password (shown once on screen) or send an
    invitation by email.
  - Roles:
      admin  everything, including settings, team, import, export, clearing
             logs
      user   daily work (users, companies, tickets, notes, calendar,
             invoices, search); no team, settings or bulk tools
  - Integrations permission: none, view or manage.
  - Disable or enable an account. A disabled account cannot sign in and open
    sessions end.
  - Reset password: sets a new temporary password and signs the person out
    everywhere.
  - Reset two-step: for a colleague who lost their phone.
  - The system always keeps at least one active admin.

21. PRIVACY PAGES
===============================================================================

Privacy policy (privacy/privacybeleid.php)
  The public text of how the organisation handles personal data.

Privacy requests (privacy/privacy.php, staff)
  1. Enter the email address of the person asking.
  2. See what is held: user record, sessions, activity-log entries and
     invoices.
  3. Download it as JSON (right of access).
  4. For erasure, tick that you checked their identity and press Anonymise.
     The name, email and country are replaced, sessions are deleted and the
     person's name in the log is replaced. Invoices are kept for the legally
     required 7 years.
  Notes, tickets and appointments are not yet part of this tool; check them
  by hand (see the security and compliance document).

23. DAILY AND BACKGROUND JOBS
===============================================================================

Set these up in the Windows Task Scheduler (or cron on Linux):

  bin/purge.php          daily. Deletes expired data: log entries over 12 months,
                     connector log entries over 90 days,
                     login attempts, rate-limit counters, monitoring history
                     over 30 days, invoices over 7 years.
                     php C:\xampp\htdocs\Websites\test\bin\purge.php
  bin/run_recurring.php  daily. Creates the recurring invoices that are due.
                     php C:\xampp\htdocs\Websites\test\bin\run_recurring.php
  bin/run_contracts.php  daily. Creates the invoices of contracts that are due,
                     renews contracts that renew by themselves, ends the
                     ones that are over.
  bin/poll_inbox.php     every 3 minutes. Reads the support mailbox and turns
                     new mail into tickets and replies (email to ticket).
  bin/check_endpoints.php  every minute; runs the endpoint checks that are due.
  bin/kuma_poll.php      every minute; keeps the Uptime history continuous when
                     nobody has the page open.

bin/purge.php, bin/run_recurring.php, bin/run_contracts.php, bin/poll_inbox.php, bin/check_endpoints.php and bin/kuma_poll.php all refuse to run from a browser.

24. WORKED EXAMPLES
===============================================================================

A. A customer calls with a problem
   1. Search the person's name. Open their page.
   2. Add a note of kind "Call" with what was said.
   3. Press "New ticket" on their page; set priority and assign it.
   4. If a visit is needed, add an appointment (their company and them as
      contact) from the panel on their page.

B. Monthly invoicing
   1. Invoices > New invoice, pick the company, add lines, save.
   2. Open it, download the PDF, send it.
   3. When money arrives, record the payment. Overdue invoices show on the
      Overview and in the bell.
   4. For the same amount every month, create a recurring invoice once.

C. Planning a week
   1. Calendar. Filter on a company if needed.
   2. Click a day to add an appointment; fill company, contact, place.
   3. Download the company's .ics and open it in your own calendar.

D. A colleague starts
   1. Settings > Team > Add team member; choose Invite by email.
   2. They open the link, choose a password, and turn on two-step.

E. A customer asks what you hold about them
   1. Privacy requests: enter their email, check the result, download the JSON.
   2. Check notes, tickets and appointments by hand (not yet automatic).
   3. Answer within one month.

F. Something looks wrong
   1. Activity logs: search the person or action.
   2. Connections: look for flagged sessions.
   3. Settings > Team: disable the account and reset the password if needed.

25. WORDS USED IN THE SYSTEM
===============================================================================

  Admin       staff role with full rights (also a customer-user role label;
              context tells which)
  Agenda      the list view of appointments
  Anonymise   replace personal details so a person can no longer be identified
  BTW         Dutch VAT
  Bell        the notification icon in the top bar
  CSRF token  the hidden code in forms that proves the form came from the
              system
  Epoch       counter used to end old sessions after a password change
  .ics        calendar file format
  KvK         Dutch chamber of commerce number
  Outbox      the record of every email, sent or not
  Recovery code  one-time code for two-step sign-in when your phone is lost
  Session     a signed-in period in one browser
  SMTP        the protocol for sending email
  TOTP        the time-based 6-digit code of an authenticator app
  Two-step    sign-in with password plus a code


===============================================================================
26. THINGS THE SYSTEM DOES NOT DO
===============================================================================

  - Customers cannot sign in or see anything themselves (no portal).
  - It does not send invoices by email by itself; you download the PDF and
    send it (email is used for notifications, reset links and invitations).
  - It does not take online payments; payments are recorded by hand.
  - It does not synchronise with Teamleader or any other ticket system yet.
  - Reports are example data at the moment.
  - It does not make backups itself; it records them.
  - It does not have an app; the web pages work on phones.
